Design & development

Built through iterative evidence-led refinement

The project evolved from lightweight IOC enrichment into a broader analyst-augmentation workflow. Each benchmark exposed a specific engineering problem: missed URLs, over-classified IOCs, ambiguous account extraction, Threat Intelligence attribution, source-specific parsing, report readability or evidence-boundary communication.

Early phase

Multi-input Streamlit application, alert parsing, IOC enrichment and basic reporting.

Phishing phase

Email authentication, URL context, HTML/brand analysis, Unicode/homoglyph detection and phishing-focused MITRE mapping.

Evidence-led phase

Canonical evidence modelling, cautious attribution, explicit confidence, observed vs malicious IOC separation and contradiction checks.

Defender validation

Nested XDR entity extraction, authentication incident routing, timeline reconstruction and report benchmark refinement.

Splunk phase

Source detection, tolerant JSON ingestion, endpoint-security-control routing and T1562.001 analysis.

Boundary phase

Explicit reporting of inaccessible telemetry, unresolved evidence and concrete further-investigation requirements.
