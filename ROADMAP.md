# Current Limitations

- Localhost / single analyst use.
- No direct live querying of Defender or Splunk.
- No automatic access to Windows Event Logs, Sysmon, firewall, VPN or change-management records unless supplied.
- Alert level reports are investigation foundations, not completed incident response investigations.
- Threat Intelligence is constrained by provider coverage, API configuration and IOC eligibility.
- Classification is evidence bound and may remain inconclusive.
- No universal accuracy claim is made from the current benchmark set.
