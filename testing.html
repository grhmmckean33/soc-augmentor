Testing & validation

Benchmarking against manually investigated incidents

SOC Augmentor has been iteratively tested by comparing generated reports with manually validated SOC investigations. Differences were used to improve extraction, evidence restraint, IOC classification, Threat Intelligence handling and report quality.

Accuracy statement: case-specific internal benchmark comparisons showed approximately 97–99% alignment on the tested alert-level outputs in mature benchmark cases. This is not a statistically validated universal product-accuracy rate and must not be interpreted as one.
BenchmarkWhat was validatedResult
Defender authentication / possible logon breachHost, account, IP roles, timestamps, severity, T1110, evidence gaps, TI attribution and report structureHigh case-level alignment
KCD Splunk — Defender disabledAlert parsing, host/time/severity, T1562.001, cautious classification, evidence boundaries and follow-up investigation guidanceHigh alert-level alignment
Phishing benchmarksAuthentication, URLs, delivery state, IOC context, MITRE and prevention/compromise distinctionContinuing validation

What the benchmark does—and does not—prove

It does show

The application can closely reproduce core facts and cautious conclusions from tested alert artefacts once adapters and logic are mature.

It does not show

That the tool completes a Tier-2 investigation, detects every attack type, or has a universal 98–99% accuracy rate.

Analyst responsibility

Generated reports are investigation foundations. Analysts must validate and corroborate against source telemetry before disposition or containment.

Regression engineering

Development used repeated regression testing after parser, routing, Threat Intelligence and report-generation changes. Recent V7.3.x builds were validated against 30+ automated regression tests covering Defender, Splunk, JSON compatibility, evidence-boundary wording and report structure.
